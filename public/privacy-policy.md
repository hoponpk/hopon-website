**\#\# 6\. DATA RETENTION**

**\#\#\# 6.1 Active Account Data**

While your account is active, we retain:

\- **\*\*Account information\*\*** (name, email, phone): For the duration of your account and as long as necessary for legal, tax, or contractual purposes  
\- **\*\*Trip history\*\***: For 7 years (to comply with tax and accounting regulations in Pakistan)  
\- **\*\*Payment records\*\***: For 7 years (to comply with financial regulations and tax requirements)  
\- **\*\*Communication records\*\***: For 2 years after the last interaction (to resolve disputes and maintain Service quality)  
\- **\*\*Device and usage analytics\*\***: For 1 year (for analytics and service improvement)  
\- **\*\*Location data\*\***: Real-time location data is deleted after Trip completion; historical location data is retained for 6 months

**\#\#\# 6.2 Deleted Account Data**

When you delete your account:

\- **\*\*Account information\*\*** will be deleted or anonymized (except as noted below)  
\- **\*\*Trip history\*\*** will be deleted (except as required for legal/tax purposes)  
\- **\*\*Communication records\*\*** will be deleted  
\- **\*\*Device and analytics data\*\*** will be deleted  
\- **\*\*Payment records\*\*** will be retained for 7 years (legal/tax requirement; they will be anonymized where possible)

**\#\#\# 6.3 Photo and Identity Document Retention After Account Deletion**

**\*\*Important\*\***: Even after you delete your account, we will retain:

\- **\*\*Profile photo\*\***: For 6 months post-deletion (to prevent duplicate account creation and fraud)  
\- **\*\*Government ID photos and verification documents\*\***: For 6 months post-deletion (to verify legitimate identity in case of disputes; to comply with potential government requests)  
\- **\*\*Vehicle registration and insurance documents\*\*** (for Drivers): For 6 months post-deletion (for potential dispute resolution)

**\*\*Reason\*\***: Fraudsters and banned Users often attempt to circumvent account suspension by creating new accounts with the same identity or vehicle. The 6-month retention period allows us to detect and prevent such behavior while respecting your privacy.

After 6 months, all photos and identity documents will be permanently deleted, unless we are legally required to retain them (e.g., for ongoing legal proceedings, government requests, or court orders).

**\#\#\# 6.4 Data Retention for Legal/Dispute Purposes**

If you are involved in an accident, a safety complaint, or a legal dispute:

\- All related data (communications, Trip details, photos, evidence) will be retained for the duration of the dispute and for 3 years thereafter to protect your legal rights and ours

**\#\#\# 6.5 Anonymized Data**

After the retention periods above, we may continue to retain anonymized or de-identified data for analytics, research, and service improvement. Anonymized data cannot identify you and is not subject to deletion requests.

**\#\#\# 7.1 Data Storage Location and Transfers**

**\#\#\# 7.2 Compliance with Data Localization Requirements**

Pakistan does not currently have a comprehensive mandatory data localization law. However:

\- We comply with applicable data protection, privacy, telecommunications, and financial regulations to the extent required by law.

**\#\#\# 7.3 Security Measures**

We implement the following technical and organizational security measures:

**\*\*Technical Controls:\*\***

\- **\*\*Encryption in Transit\*\***: All data in transit between your device and our servers uses TLS/SSL encryption (minimum TLS 1.2)  
\- **\*\*Encryption at Rest\*\***: Sensitive data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption  
\- **\*\*Password Security\*\***: User passwords are hashed using bcrypt or similar strong algorithms; we do not store plaintext passwords  
\- **\*\*Access Controls\*\***: Role-based access control (RBAC) limits employee access to personal data  
\- **\*\*Database Security\*\***: Databases are isolated, firewalled, and monitored for intrusion  
\- **\*\*API Security\*\***: APIs use OAuth 2.0 and API key authentication to prevent unauthorized access  
\- **\*\*DDoS Protection\*\***: Infrastructure is protected against distributed denial-of-service attacks

**\*\*Organizational Controls:\*\***

\- **\*\*Data Protection Training\*\***: All employees handling personal data receive privacy and security training  
\- **\*\*Access Audit Logs\*\***: All access to personal data is logged and monitored  
\- **\*\*Incident Response Plan\*\***: We maintain a plan to respond to security breaches  
\- **\*\*Third-Party Audits\*\***: We conduct or commission regular security audits of our systems  
\- **\*\*Data Minimization\*\***: We minimize data access on a need-to-know basis

**\*\*Limitations:\*\***

\- No security system is impenetrable; we cannot guarantee 100% security  
\- Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their passwords and account credentials  
\- Users should not share login credentials with others

While we use commercially reasonable safeguards, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

**\#\#\# 7.4 Breach Notification**

In the event of a data breach that compromises personal data:

\- We will investigate the breach and notify affected Users without unreasonable delay  
\- Notification will be via email to the address associated with your account  
\- Notification will include: nature of breach, data affected, steps we are taking, and steps you can take to protect yourself  
\- We will cooperate with relevant government authorities and regulatory bodies as required
